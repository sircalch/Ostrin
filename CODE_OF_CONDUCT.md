# Code of Conduct

The Ostrin project is committed to a welcoming, respectful and technically
focused community.

Participants are expected to communicate with patience, focus criticism on
ideas and code, explain disagreements with evidence, welcome different
backgrounds and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content or
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through the contact
method listed in the repository. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
possible.

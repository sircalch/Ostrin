## Method

- External slope: each workload runs with K = 1 and K = 5, paired per repetition (7 repetitions).
- Order of the builds is rotated at every repetition. Each build's RESULT line is checked before its times are used.
- "faster/slower (conclusive)": the paired-slope ranges do not overlap. "same within noise": they overlap.
- The baseline is the first build (B0). Times are wall-clock per run of the workload, including the interpreter's start-up cost, which the slope removes.

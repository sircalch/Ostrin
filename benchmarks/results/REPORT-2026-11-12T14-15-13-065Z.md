## Method

- Correctness is checked before timing, against references computed independently (analytic values or JavaScript).
- `total` is the median wall time of one run, including process start; `init` is the median of an empty program; `run ≈` is `total − init` (estimate).
- Dispersion: minimum, maximum and median absolute deviation (MAD) over the repetitions.
- A `run ≈` value smaller than the larger MAD of the two measurements is below the measurement resolution and is not interpreted.
- Ostrin has no clock inside a program, so the kernel time is always estimated from outside the process.

- Wall-clock timing includes process start and the operating system's scheduling; the results on this machine do not generalise to other machines.
- No Wolfram or MATLAB results are included.
